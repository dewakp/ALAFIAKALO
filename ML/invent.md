 The human body system is a complex bio-chemical plant where various reactions take place. These reactions, or chains of reactions are often characterized as sets of bio-chemical pathways. Indeed, a better characterization will be electro-bio-chemical pathways. These pathways have intermediate and final outcomes or outputs. It is these outputs that govern our physiological characteristics, states or symptoms, depending on the context.

Understanding the various electro-bio-chemical pathways (EBC) and their intermediate and final states (outcomes) is a missing piece in modern medical and pharmacological practices. Various external factors interact with and impact the EBC and thus their outcomes. Examples of external factors include pathogens, nutrition, medical therapies (including medications), activities, neural inputs, environmental chemical and electro-magnetic factors, etc. The human bio-chemical system occupies different states at different times depending on the underlying factors and various inputs.

    Our goal is to characterize the human-electro--bio-chemical system (HEBCS) using a set of algorithms. 
    We will then develop a learning system for interactions with external factors based on different states of being. 
    Our final objective is to quantify the various possible states for any given person.
    The output of this work is a universally applicable wellness score that informs of a person state of wellness at any given time and can provide insight into future state given certain inputs.
