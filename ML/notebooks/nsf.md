
Intellectual Merit and Technical Innovation:

Development of a mathematical and quantitative representation for bio-chemical and neurological pathways, towards the development of a wellness score. The score will be based on a combination of metrics derived from the analysis of aggregated individual and population group health and wellness data. The score will give an indication of a user’s wellness on a spectrum and can serve as basis for predictive guidance of future behaviors such as what and when to eat, do specific activities, take medications and so on. 

Bio-chemical pathways such as endocrine-pathways, circulatory-pathways, digestive-pathways; as well as electromagnetic pathways including neurological pathways, and so on, provide an essential framework for cause-and-effect analysis for the relationships between various input triggers and human physiological or wellness state. A mathematical model of pathways will offer unique insight that could assist in clinical settings as well as for making ongoing life decisions.

On a population level it can serve as a basis for issues surveillance and identification. This idea is the result of more than one decade of experience with data analytics and metrics development for information technology systems, and unique set of experiences with chronic health conditions. After collecting data from genetics, nutrition, medications, clinical lab, activities, environmental, and mental state, we observed it was easier to predict future state depending on any given combination of these data points and was able to improve my overall well-being and clinical lab outcomes. 

We also observed that most times, the data driven observations are far more prescient than conventional wisdom dictates. So, we developed a rough algorithm for a wellness score. This funding will help investigate this further using more data and computational resources to explore the role of multi-modal, multi-dimensional machine learning and artificial intelligence algorithms.

While developing the wellness score, we will explore the use of privacy preserving tools for a distributed source machine learning environment as a template for deployment to end user. 


Research Work and Expected Outcome:

The human system is a collection of sub-systems and biochemical pathways, each working as part of a collective to determine the overall wellness of a person. Biochemical pathways respond to various stimuli to effect physiological wellbeing, each pathway outcome triggering other pathways in a feedback loop. Understanding the interactions between these various pathways and external triggers such as micro-nutrients, activities, medications, and other environmental factors will help us develop a wellness score that can then be used to inform a person about the state of their health, and potentially predict a future state given a set of triggers. This could have a big impact on the decisions people make about various health impacting conditions. It could also help inform various population health decisions. 

As part of this work, we will develop an aggregated database of all biochemical pathways and known triggers. We will then develop, using machine learning algorithms a cause and effect mapping for all triggers. We will develop a set of metrics for wellness and explore a metric for wellness as the outcome. We will use de-identified individual and population datasets to identify and validate the set of wellness metrics and the wellness score.

We will explore the use of existing machine learning infrastructure such as IBM Watson, Keras on TensorFlow, and others.  We explore both a centralized ML model and also a fully decentralized one for their privacy preserving and autonomous values. 


Market Opportunity:

Quantifying biochemical, neurological and other physiological pathways will open new opportunities in pharmacology, neurology, and other medical technologies, helping pave a path to personalized medicine. As a digital health focused company, we see opportunities in a wellness score as a key to several digital health features that would help our users live a healthier life by making better life choices based on informed and prescient advice.


Broader Impact    
 
With the advent of health information systems and smart devices that can track activities, nutrition and vitals, users are open to the concept of monitoring their health and wellness information. Millions of people in the United States, and billions globally suffer from some chronic disease or another. Knowing one’s overall wellness and what may trigger an adverse event is not usually intuitive. Having a digital assistant that can help make those deductions at any moment will be of great value to individuals. We see a universal utility for a wellness score focused application that also offers an aggregated user interface for the underlying biomedical data. Potential features of the application could include food adviser, activity adviser, medication-compliment notifier, and automated medical diagnosis. A fully mapped biochemical pathway database could be of value to the pharmaceutical industry and for disease modeling.

Codependent Multivariate Analytics

 The goal of this project is to develop a robust algorithm that produces a wellness score. The algorithm will be used to metricize the human bio-electro-chemical framework. It is well established that the human system consists of and is regulated by myriads of bio-electro-chemical elements. A number of bio-chemical pathways have been studied and documented. In this project, we will collate all existing pathways, explore any outstanding pathways, and quantify the pathways and their intermediary states.

Preliminary studies of the hemolytic pathway, the gastrointestinal pathway and the metabolic pathway have offered therapeutic solutions for a g6pd deficient chronic kidney disease patient on dialysis. This study was based on data for one patient. The first phase of this SBIR project will expand this study to an additional up to ten patients with similar disease conditions.

Data used for this project will include lifestyle data such as complete daily nutritional intake, expanded blood sample results, clinical notes, daily vitals collected several times during each day, dialysis therapies flowsheet data, daily medication intakes and activities data.

This project will research several machine learning algorithms towards the determination of a model or a set of models. The goal is a universally applicable set of metrics for the development of a wellness score.

We will work with a University IRB as necessary to assure the privacy and integrity of privilege personal health information and data. When personal health and wellness data is required, we will obtain consent from the prospective subjects consistent with HIPAA requirements and IRB guidance.

A team that includes highly regarded physicians and nutritionists will work with the principal investigator or cohort identification and recruitment. The team will also be the focal point for data collection and interpretation.

The principal investigator will lead data storage, processing, analysis and modelling.
