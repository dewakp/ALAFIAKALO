This is fundamentally a deta collection HUB. It collects data about a person such as what they consume (food, drinks, medications), what they discharge(poop, urine, vomit), what they do (physical activities), what they think (journaling, thoughts), symptoms (injuries, feelings, pain), Sleep.

It overlays these on ground truths. Biology (Age, Sex, Height, Body Structure, Weight, Lab Results, Genetic Data), Hospital Notes, Medical Therapies and Treatments (Dialysis, Chemotherapy, etc), Environmental and Social Factors.

It identifies patterns, relationship graphs, cause and effects.

It predicts future outcomes (states)

It calculate scores vs expected outcomes.

It interacts via Agents.

Features: Interfaces are infered from commands which are based on devices being used --- voice, images, video, text, documents, other tools.

Use prompt as entry point. voice, text or image will determin interface to surface. prompt will respond to text, voice and click camera icon next to prompt window will let user upload or take new pictures. 

Example Workflows:
  Web: Login to App. See Prompt Page.
                         Start speaking, Voice AI Kicks in
                         Start Typing, LLM Kicks in
                         Click on Camera, Video/Image AI Kicks in
  Mobile: Starts with a Prompt Page
                        Same as Web
