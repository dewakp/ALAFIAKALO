Nutrition that understands your condition
Log a meal by typing it, speaking it, or photographing it. ALAFIA estimates the nutrients that matter for you — protein, potassium, phosphorus, sodium — against targets calculated from your own body and diagnoses, not a generic table.
Labs and results, in one timeline
Upload a lab report or clinic PDF and ALAFIA reads it, including the reference ranges, and shows you how each value has moved over time. Nothing is added to your record until you confirm it.
Medications, tracked properly
Record what you actually took, not just what was prescribed. ALAFIA checks the name and the dose as you enter them, so a slip of the finger doesn’t become part of your medical record.
Activity, treatment and how you feel
Exercise, sleep, symptoms and mood alongside dialysis or other treatment sessions — so patterns show up where they otherwise wouldn’t.
Share with your physician and the people who help you
Give your nephrologist, GP or a family carer access to exactly what you choose — and withdraw it whenever you want. They see a clear picture instead of trying to reconstruct months from memory at an appointment.
Secure messaging, inside the app
Message your care team without health details ending up in ordinary email or a chat app that was never built for them.
Your data, protected on purpose
Your identity is separated from your clinical details before anything is processed, AI features are opt-in and can be switched off at any time, and you can export or delete your record whenever you decide to.
And a health assistant that knows your record
Ask it anything — whether a meal fits your limits, what a result means, how your week has gone. It answers from your labs, your medications and your sessions, which is what makes it different from searching the internet. It is support and guidance, not a substitute for your clinician.
